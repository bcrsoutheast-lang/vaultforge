Smart Deal Routing Engine

When investor deal submissions open, opportunities will not be blasted to everyone. VaultForge will route deals to the right buyers, lenders, and partners based on fit.

Deal enters the system

Property details, numbers, timeline, issue type, and opportunity category are captured inside the platform.

Opportunity gets classified

The system identifies whether it fits flip buyers, rental buyers, funding partners, rescue operators, title review, or other strategic routes.

Matched partners get notified

Buyers and partners receive targeted alerts by market, price range, asset type, strategy, and urgency. No cold mass blasts.

Targeted Buyer Alerts

A flip deal should reach flippers. A rental should reach landlords. An urgent close should reach fast-close buyers. That is the core difference between random lists and real routing.

Instant Network Notifications

Qualified matches will be notified by text and system alerts, creating fast responses without burning out the network with irrelevant deals.
